Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on caring for natural jute, delivery times, and custom printing.

How do I clean and care for my handcrafted jute floor rug or mats? +

Natural jute is naturally stain-resistant and easy to maintain. Simply vacuum regularly or shake gently outside to remove loose dust. For spills, blot immediately with a clean, dry cloth or damp sponge without scrubbing. Avoid soaking in water or machine washing to preserve fiber tightness.

Are your jute tote bags water-resistant? +

Yes! Our tote bags and shopping bags feature a thin, biodegradable food-safe inner moisture membrane that prevents liquid leakage and allows easy wipe-cleaning with a damp cloth, while keeping the exterior 100% natural and unbleached.

What are the shipping timelines across India? +

All orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours from our Kolkata fulfillment center. Metro cities generally receive delivery in 3 to 4 business days; other regions in 4 to 6 business days with SMS and Email tracking updates.

Can we order custom sizes and branded logos for corporate events? +
